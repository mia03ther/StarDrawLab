# StarDraw Billing

StarDraw offers consumable creative credits alongside time-bound membership access. The two product systems share payment and accounting controls but grant different entitlements.

## Language

**Permanent Point Package**:
A one-time product that grants non-expiring points without changing the buyer's membership tier.
_Avoid_: Subscription, membership package

**Membership Subscription**:
A monthly or yearly product that grants a membership tier and its configured point entitlements for a billing period.
_Avoid_: Point package, recharge package

**Purchased Points**:
Points obtained as part of a paid product. Unused purchased points carry forward and do not expire.
_Avoid_: Monthly bonus

**Monthly Bonus Points**:
Recurring membership points granted for the current monthly cycle and expiring at the end of that cycle.
_Avoid_: Purchased points, permanent points

**Point Lot**:
An attributable balance grant whose source and expiry determine consumption and refund eligibility.
_Avoid_: Account balance
